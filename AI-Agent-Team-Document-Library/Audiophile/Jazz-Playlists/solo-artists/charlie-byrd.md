**Notes:**
- *Jazz Samba* (1962, with Stan Getz) is the landmark album that ignited
  the American bossa nova craze — Byrd arranged the tracks after hearing
  bossa nova during a 1961 tour of Brazil.
- Also a classically trained guitarist — recorded solo classical/Brazilian
  repertoire (Villa-Lobos) alongside his jazz work.
- Strong fit for **Poolside Evenings** and **Strings & Wood** (Guitar).
