**Notes:**
- "Blue Bossa" (his most famous composition, from *Una Mas*) is one of the
  most-covered jazz standards ever written — strong **Audiophile Trumpet**
  anchor. Note: same tune title also appears on Joe Henderson's *Page One*
  (Henderson was a sideman on Dorham's original recording too) — these are
  distinct performances, both legitimate to include on different playlists.
