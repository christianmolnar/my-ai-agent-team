**Notes:**
- "Nardis" and "Gloria's Step" are from the celebrated Scott LaFaro/Paul Motian
  trio sessions (Village Vanguard, June 1961) — arguably the most acclaimed
  Evans trio recordings ever made.
- "Waltz for Debby" and "My Foolish Heart" are both drawn from the same 1961
  Vanguard sessions but released across the two companion LPs (*Sunday at the
  Village Vanguard* and *Waltz for Debby*).
- Candidate for **Piano Trios** (Small Groups) as well as **Piano After Dark**
  and **Audiophile Piano**.
