**Notes:**

  and his intensely emotional late-1970s comeback recordings — both eras
  represented here.
- "Meets the Rhythm Section" (1957, with the Miles Davis rhythm section) is a
  landmark album — candidate for **Legendary Duos/Collaborations** context
  even though billed as Pepper solo.
