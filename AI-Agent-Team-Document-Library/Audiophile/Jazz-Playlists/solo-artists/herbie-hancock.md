**Notes:**
Hancock spans nearly every era of modern jazz — hard-bop/post-bop as a Blue Note leader and member of the Miles Davis Second Great Quintet, the funk/fusion breakthrough of the Headhunters, 1980s electro-pop crossover ("Rockit"), and later career Grammy-winning vocal-collaboration albums. All tracks/albums verified against Wikipedia's Herbie Hancock discography and biography.

- "Watermelon Man" and "Cantaloupe Island" are foundational jazz-funk standards, later sampled widely in hip-hop.
- "Maiden Voyage" and "Dolphin Dance" represent his modal/post-bop writing at its most lyrical.
- "Chameleon" and "Sly" are the core Head Hunters tracks that crossed over to pop/funk audiences.
