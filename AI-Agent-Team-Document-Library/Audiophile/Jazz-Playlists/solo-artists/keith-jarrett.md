**Notes:**
- *The Köln Concert* (1975) is Jarrett's best-selling and most acclaimed solo
  piano record — an entirely improvised concert. "Part I" alone runs ~20+
  minutes; the CD/streaming split into "Part I", "Part II a", "Part II b",
  "Part II c" varies by reissue, so double-check exact track naming on the
  platform at import time.
- *Standards, Vol. 1 & 2* and *My Song* feature his long-running trio with
  Gary Peacock (bass) and Jack DeJohnette (drums) — a strong "conversational"
  trio pick, good candidate cross-list for **Friends & Conversations** too.
- *The Melody at Night, with You* is intimate solo piano, recorded during
  recovery from chronic fatigue syndrome — a strong **Piano After Dark** /
  **Audiophile Piano** anchor.
- Candidate for **Piano After Dark**, **Audiophile Piano**, and **Piano Trios**
  (Small Groups) depending on track chosen.
