**Notes:**
- *Time Out* (1959) is one of the best-selling and most historically
  significant jazz albums ever recorded, built entirely around experiments
  in unusual time signatures — "Take Five" (5/4) is one of the most
  recognizable jazz recordings of all time. Core anchor for **Quartets**
  (Small Groups).
- Paul Desmond composed "Take Five"; both his file and this one legitimately
  reference it, since it's credited to the Dave Brubeck Quartet as a group
  recording.
