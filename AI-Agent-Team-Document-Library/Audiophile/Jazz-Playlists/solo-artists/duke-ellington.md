**Notes:**
Widely regarded as America's greatest jazz composer/bandleader — 50+ years leading his orchestra, with longtime writing partner Billy Strayhorn ("Take the 'A' Train" is a Strayhorn composition and became the band's signature theme). All tracks/albums verified against Wikipedia's Duke Ellington biography.

- "Take the 'A' Train," "Mood Indigo," "Sophisticated Lady," and "In a Sentimental Mood" are cornerstone Great American Songbook standards.
- "Diminuendo and Crescendo in Blue" (Paul Gonsalves's 27-chorus solo) sparked Ellington's famous 1956 Newport comeback.
- "Money Jungle" is a rare, more modern/avant-leaning session with Charles Mingus and Max Roach.
